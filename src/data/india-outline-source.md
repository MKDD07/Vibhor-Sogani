# India boundary

Source: Survey of India, Outline of India (1:16 million).
https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/outline-maps-of-india
Download: https://surveyofindia.gov.in/documents/Outline_of_India.zip

Converted from the supplied Lambert Conformal Conic projection to WGS84 using mapshaper; simplified to 0.5% with keep-shapes. Ring winding was normalized for d3-geo and coordinates rounded to five decimals. This is a generalized display outline, not survey precision data.

ExhibitionMap masks the underlying Natural Earth polygons beneath this outline and draws this geometry in their place. The local file is shared by the home and About maps.
